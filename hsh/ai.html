Astra does NOT think!

No AI can actually think!

When using AI, the text "Thinking..." might be seen when the AI is generating its answer to your request, this is the case for ChatGPT, or even Astra (which is based on GPT).

Though it is not actually thinking. It gives the illusion that it is thinking.

When an AI shows "Thinking...", it's predicting the next words based on patterns in data. It doesn't understand, feel, or reason, it just calculates what sounds right. The term "thinking" is just a way to say "Hey, the AI is doing some very complex calculations to answer your question".
